## Summary
Paper V of Ljung's licentiate. Operationalises model-based construction (Total BIM) as an
integration of BIM, Lean Construction and Total Quality Management (VDC–Lean–TQM), with the
model as the production-ready source of truth.

## Key takeaways (from the thesis summary)
- Two cases: a **Norwegian hospital** and a **Swedish office**; focus groups, site
  observations, interviews. Analytical lens: **MMI**, **LOIN**, **Systematic Completion**.
- Hospital: SC quality gates and weekly **17–0 checkpoints** let model maturity progress in
  step with procurement, **control zones** and takt-time planning.
- Office, with fragmented procurement and no early contractor involvement: weaker alignment
  between model development and production needs.
- Across cases: when maturity, QA and production planning line up, the reported benefits are
  less rework, shorter schedules, better cost predictability (qualitative).

## Distinct contribution
The model-maturity and quality-gate side of the Chalmers programme: how information
maturity is governed against control zones and takt, where the TBS papers concern how the
information is structured.
